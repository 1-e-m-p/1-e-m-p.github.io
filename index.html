About

I'm a sophomore studying mechanical engineering at the University of Maryland. The design process is a big part of why I'm in engineering — specifically the middle of it, where a first attempt fails and working out why produces something better than the original idea.

Product design engineering is what I'm working toward: the role between how something looks and how it actually gets manufactured. Right now that means getting fast in CAD, learning machining hands-on, and finding shop work where I can learn how parts are actually made, and use that to understand what's possible and what's off the table when I'm the one designing a product.
